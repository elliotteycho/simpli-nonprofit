# Project Overview

## What Simpli is

Simpli is a full stack platform that helps large corporations donate excess inventory to charities. It handles vetting, logistics, and impact reporting. Companies such as Cargill, L'Oreal, and Glow Recipe run donation pilots through it. To date the platform has facilitated roughly $70M in donations, and the goal for this year is $150M or more.

Simpli also owns SearchCharities.org, a charity directory with detailed profiles, IRS filing data, and program information. The activation layer in this project builds on top of those existing pages rather than introducing a new product surface.

## The project

This was an eight week Product Space x Simpli collaboration. The brief was to design and prototype a new charity facing feature that plugs into Simpli's existing platform, with the understanding that Simpli's engineering team would build the production version if the pitch was strong.

Elliott Cho led the project. The PM team included Anikait Rawat and Vyom Mishra. Simpli was represented by Tommy, the CEO, and Joseph Oliver, an engineer rebuilding the onboarding flow.

## The problem in one line

Simpli can bring in corporate supply faster than its charity network can absorb it, because most charities never get activated.

The full breakdown is in `problem-statement.md`. In short, a production audit of 198 charities surfaced a three layered gap: 55 percent never complete onboarding, 77 percent have never received a donation, and the onboarded base needs to roughly double to meet supply targets.

## The solution

An activation layer that wraps Simpli's onboarding and the period around it. Three ideas carry it:

1. Lead with value. Show a charity its potential match data and local impact before asking it to fill out forms.
2. Make completion legible and rewarding. A match readiness score turns a 13 section profile into a clear, gamified path.
3. Make the donation match moment frictionless. Collect warehouse and logistics details just in time, when a charity is accepting a real donation, not upfront.

This maps directly onto the three phase onboarding rebuild that Joseph was already building: intro call, simplified on platform step, and donation match. The team's contribution is the engagement and retention experience around those phases, not the onboarding form itself.

## Deliverables

- Secondary research brief on nonprofit pain points and the competitive landscape
- Two production platform audits that established the real funnel data
- A product requirements document for the activation layer
- A five screen interactive prototype
- A presentation deck delivered to Simpli leadership

## Status

Discovery and the PRD are complete. The prototype is built and demonstrated. The work was presented to Simpli leadership in April 2026.
