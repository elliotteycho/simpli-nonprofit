2 Evidence Base

Cross-referenced from dev environment audit (April 7), secondary research brief (April 5), Reddit deep threads (4 full text), Quora practitioner interviews, and Tommy's original project brief (Feb 21).

Platform Audit (April 7)

48.5% pending in MissionControl. Only 30% of on-platform charities vetted. 89.7% come through Simpli invite (not self-signup). No charity-facing portal exists. Charity profiles average 3/9 fields. Joseph actively building text message flows for donation acceptance.

Tommy's Original Brief (Feb 21)

"Putting aside the free stuff angle." Tommy's central challenge: onboarding charities is the bottleneck. He asked for tools that motivate charities to onboard before donations. Mentioned a Linktree-like microsite, events management, volunteer tools, impact dashboard.

Secondary Research (87+ Sources)

Strongest signal: data fragmentation and CRM lock-in. "Tighter governance beats more tools" (r/nonprofit consensus). Any new login faces adoption headwinds. 78% of donors unaware of matching. Double the Donation saw 0 ROI after 3 years (top-voted Reddit). Push > Pull.

Key Constraint

Joseph is actively building the text/SMS notification layer for charity allocation acceptance (3 test orders April 6-7). Our solution must complement that work, not duplicate it. The microsite solves the step BEFORE Joseph's flow: getting charities activated so they can receive those texts.

5 The Solution: Charity Microsite

A public-facing, Linktree-style microsite ("Powered by Simpli") for each charity partner. One page. No login required to view. Completing the profile unlocks donation match eligibility.

What It Is

A single-page charity profile that shows the charity's mission, impact stats, donation history with Simpli, and a public link they can share with donors, board members, and grant funders. Think Linktree for nonprofits, but connected to real donation data.

Why Charities Care

Immediate value before any donation: A shareable page they can put in their email signature, grant applications, and social bios. Impact reporting is one of their biggest pain points (confirmed by 4 Reddit threads). This page auto-generates it.

Why Simpli Cares

Profile completion is the gate: To unlock the full microsite (and donation match eligibility), charities must complete their profile fields. This naturally moves them from 3/9 to 9/9 without Simpli nagging. Data quality improves by design.

How It Connects to Joseph's Work

Microsite = activation. Joseph's SMS = transaction. The microsite gets charities onboarded and profile-complete. Joseph's text notifications handle the donation acceptance flow after they're active. Complementary, not competing.

8 Two MVPs for April 19

Both deliverables must be presentable at the final presentation. They demonstrate the full charity journey from invite to activation.

MVP 1

Coded Charity Microsite

Format: Functional HTML/CSS/JS prototype (not Figma)

Pages: Complete profile view + incomplete profile view with progress bar

Data: Populated with real Hope Mission Center data from dev environment

Interactive: Clickable checklist items, progress bar animation, share button

Why it matters: Shows Tommy exactly what charities would see and interact with. A coded prototype carries more weight than mockups because it proves feasibility.

MVP 2

Onboarding Email Sequence

Format: 3-email HTML mockup with Simpli branding and clickable elements

Email 1 (Day 0): "Your charity page is live" with microsite link and 3/9 progress preview

Email 2 (Day 3): "You're 3 steps away from donations" with specific missing fields

Email 3 (Day 7): "Charities like you received $48K last quarter" with social proof from active partners

Why it matters: The microsite only works if charities visit it. The email sequence is the activation mechanism that replaces Simpli's current "Send Reminders" button with a designed funnel.

11 Risks and Mitigations

Risk 1: Tommy/Joseph don't confirm microsite direction

Impact: High. We build something that conflicts with active engineering work.

Mitigation: Send the confirmation message by April 9. If no response by April 10 noon, pivot the microsite framing to "onboarding activation concept" which is direction-agnostic and still presentable.

Risk 2: Zero primary user interviews

Impact: Medium. Weakens credibility of solution at presentation.

Mitigation: The dev environment audit provides real platform data as a substitute for interview data. Frame the microsite as a data-informed hypothesis to be validated, not a confirmed solution. Ask Tommy for warm introductions to 2 active charity partners.

Risk 3: Prototype scope creep

Impact: Medium. Vyom tries to build too many features and misses the April 13 deadline.

Mitigation: Scope is locked to two views (complete + incomplete profile). No backend, no auth, no database. Static HTML with hardcoded data from the dev environment. Working > polished.

Risk 4: Email sequence feels generic

Impact: Low. Reduces impact of MVP 2 at presentation.

Mitigation: Use real charity data (Hope Mission Center stats) and real Simpli branding. Personalization is the differentiator. Nancy should pull copy language from the secondary research brief, especially the "what's in it for me" framing.
