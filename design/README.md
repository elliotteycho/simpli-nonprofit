# Design

## microsite-blueprint.html

This is the blueprint for the interim microsite direction, a standalone Linktree style charity page that was explored between April 7 and April 12. It was superseded when the team realized SearchCharities.org already had detailed charity profiles, and that an activation layer on top of those existing pages was a stronger play than a new public page.

It is kept here because it documents real product thinking and shows the lineage from the microsite idea to the activation layer. The current direction lives in `../prototype/`.
