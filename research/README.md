# Research

The evidence base for the activation layer. Every claim in the problem statement traces back to one of these files.

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `secondary-research-brief.md` | Secondary research on nonprofit pain points, the tool landscape, and the competitive gap. Sourced from industry reports, nonprofit technology writing, and nonprofit community threads |
| `prod-vs-dev-audit-apr12.md` | April 12 audit comparing Simpli's production and dev environments. Established that dev data was inflated test data and production numbers are the only reliable source |
| `production-audit-apr20.md` | April 20 full audit of all 198 charities on production. Source of the funnel data, the three layered gap, the pilot figures, and the onboarding data model |

## Headline findings

- Production has 198 charities. 55 percent never complete onboarding, 77 percent have never received a donation.
- Six active company pilots represent more than $4.57M in known value and over 297 pallets.
- Onboarding is a 13 section profile form. Completeness is poor even among active charities.
- The "all charities closed" claim from an earlier read was wrong. Open and Closed refer to operating hours only.
